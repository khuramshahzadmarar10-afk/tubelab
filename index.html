TubeLab is one system for the whole job: find a niche with real demand, pull the exact words people search, build the title and hook, design and test the thumbnail, then watch your channels and your competitors — in one place.

Free. No sign-up. Your data stays in your browser.

Niche→Keyword→Title & Hook→Thumbnail→Check & Test→Watch Rivals

Niche Finder

Type a broad topic. TubeLab pulls what people actually search around it and scores each niche: Demand (how much search surrounds it), Depth (how many sub-topics exist) and Intent (how specific the searches are — specific usually clicks better).

Keyword Finder

The exact phrases people type into YouTube, pulled live from YouTube's own suggestions. Use the winning phrase as your title's first words — that is what the search bar rewards.

Content Studio

Give it a topic (best: a keyword phrase from the Keyword Finder). It builds titles on winner patterns, a first-10-seconds hook that shows the topic itself, and a script outline with beats. If you add an AI key in Settings, the AI button writes fuller versions.

Thumbnail Maker

Design the whole thumbnail inside TubeLab. One dominant subject, a short hook, designed lettering — the pattern that wins the click. Export at full 1280×720.

Tip: one face or one object filling the frame beats a busy scene. Let the picture pose the problem; the title explains it.

Thumbnail Checker

Upload up to 3 variants. TubeLab shows each at real feed sizes on a phone, measures brightness, contrast and visual clutter, and gives each a click-readiness score — the same checks to run before a Studio A/B test.

My Channels

Your whole fleet on one screen: latest uploads and public view counts, straight from YouTube's public feeds. Hit refresh on any channel — or all of them.

Competitor Watch

Add rival channels and TubeLab keeps their latest videos here, flagging breakouts — videos performing far above that channel's own normal numbers. A breakout is a proven topic: study it, then beat it with your own angle.

Settings

TubeLab works fully without any key. Keys only unlock the AI writer in Content Studio, and they are stored only in this browser — never sent anywhere except the provider you chose.

Your data

Watched competitors, added channels and cached channel feeds live in this browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a TubeLab server — there isn't one.

How it works

Keywords & niches come from YouTube's own public suggestion feed, fetched live. Channel and competitor videos come from YouTube's public RSS feeds (through a public CORS helper when the browser needs one). Thumbnails are designed and measured entirely on your device with the canvas. Scores are transparent heuristics — the breakdown is always shown, never a black box.
